💊 Actionable Agricultural Management & Dosage Recommendations

🛡️ Chemical Intervention & Dosage

Chlorothalonil 75 WP @ 2.0 g/L

Apply via foliar spray ensuring thorough coverage of both upper and lower leaf surfaces. Repeat after 10 days if symptoms persist.

🌱 Cultural Management Practices

Avoid overhead sprinkler irrigation to reduce leaf wetness duration. Prune lower infected leaves and dispose off-field. Ensure 45cm row spacing for canopy aeration.
